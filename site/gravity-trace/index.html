Bend the trace, not the map.

Tracers enter from the west edge. Place temporary gravity wells to curve their path through the rust-colored survey gate.

  • Hold longer for a stronger well.

01 / MODEL

Inverse-square, softened.

Each placed well applies a bounded inverse-square attraction. Softening near the center keeps the integration stable and playable rather than singular.

02 / LIMIT

A field, not astrophysics.

The simulation is an educational toy: no relativistic effects, no mutual particle gravity, and no claim of physical scale fidelity.

03 / SOURCE

Original, dependency-free code.

Canvas rendering, physics, input, sound, and UI are implemented from scratch with browser APIs. The game source is released under the MIT License.
