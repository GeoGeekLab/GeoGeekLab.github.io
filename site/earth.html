03 / OBSERVATION CONDITIONS

A map is not evidence until its conditions are visible.

Every active layer carries four minimum declarations. They are part of the instrument, not documentation hidden elsewhere.

SOURCEWho measured it?

Provider, instrument or network.

TIMEWhen is it valid?

Observation, forecast or event time.

RESOLUTIONWhat can it resolve?

Pixel, station, model or reporting scale.

LIMITWhat does it not mean?

Uncertainty, proxy and interpretation boundary.
