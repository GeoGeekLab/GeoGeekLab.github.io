## Editorial position

This record should not become a forty-year summary of United States history.

The useful question is narrower:

> What changed in the reader's orientation after moving through Manchester's long historical panorama?

The book's scale makes several editorial directions plausible, but none should be asserted before the reader confirms them.

## 03 / SHIFT

Choose one primary shift only after the reading effect is stated.

Candidate diagnostic questions:

### FRAME

Did the book move the frame from official political history toward a simultaneous field of politics, economics, media, fashion, technology, public mood, and ordinary life?

### SCALE

Did the forty-year span change how individual events are judged by placing them inside longer continuities from the Depression through the postwar order and into the early 1970s?

### DISTANCE

Did the dense narrative make familiar events feel less like settled landmarks and more like unstable experiences lived without knowledge of what came next?

### VOCABULARY

Did the book give you a more precise way to distinguish national myth, public mood, political performance, material life, and historical memory?

### METHOD

Did Manchester's montage-like movement between leaders, institutions, media, objects, habits, and scenes change how you think a historical period can be reconstructed?
